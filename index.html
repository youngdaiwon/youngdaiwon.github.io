I am an Assistant Professor of Marketing at the Charles F. Dolan School of Business, Fairfield University. My research examines consumer decision making and the psychological processes that shape how people evaluate products, information, and emerging technologies. I am particularly interested in topics at the intersection of consumer behavior, digital marketing, language, technology, and cognitive psychology. Much of my work uses experimental methods to identify the mechanisms underlying consumer judgments, preferences, and behavior.
